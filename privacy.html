GHOSTFACE BOT

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ghostface Discord Bot ("Ghostface", "the Bot", "we", or "our") collects, uses, and stores information when users interact with the Bot.

By using Ghostface, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Ghostface may collect and store information necessary to provide its features and maintain user progress.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server (guild) IDs.
  • Bot-created player profiles.
  • Experience points and player levels.
  • Virtual currency balances.
  • Virtual inventories, equipment, and items.
  • RPG statistics and character progression.
  • Game statistics, streaks, scores, and achievements.
  • Bot preferences or settings associated with a user or server.
  • Information necessary for cooldowns and other Bot functionality.

Discord IDs are used to associate stored Bot data with the appropriate Discord user or server.

2. Discord Information

When interacting with Ghostface, certain information may be provided to the Bot through Discord's platform and API.

Depending on the feature being used and the permissions granted to the Bot, this may include information such as your Discord user ID, username, display name, server ID, channel ID, role information, and information related to interactions with Bot commands.

Ghostface does not request your Discord password or login credentials.

3. Message Content

Some Ghostface features may process Discord messages when necessary to provide functionality such as commands, games, activity-based features, or progression systems.

Message content processed for these features is not intended to be permanently stored unless a specific Bot feature requires information derived from that interaction to function.

4. How Information Is Used

Information collected by Ghostface may be used to:

  • Operate Bot commands and features.
  • Maintain player profiles and progression.
  • Maintain virtual currency and inventory balances.
  • Provide RPG and game functionality.
  • Track game statistics and streaks.
  • Enforce cooldowns and prevent abuse.
  • Maintain server-specific Bot functionality.
  • Diagnose bugs and technical problems.
  • Protect the Bot against abuse, exploits, or manipulation.
  • Improve Ghostface and its features.

5. Sharing of Information

Ghostface does not sell users' personal information.

Bot-related information is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, protect the security of the Bot or its users, investigate abuse, or protect our rights.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by Ghostface against unauthorized access, modification, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Ghostface's functionality, maintain game progression, prevent abuse, and operate the Bot.

Data may also be removed when it is no longer necessary, when a feature is discontinued, or when Bot data is reset.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of data associated with their Discord account that is stored by Ghostface.

Data deletion requests can be made by contacting the Ghostface Bot developer through the official Ghostface Discord server.

Users requesting deletion may be asked to verify ownership of the Discord account associated with the requested data.

9. Third-Party Services

Ghostface operates through Discord and may rely on hosting, infrastructure, APIs, or other services necessary to operate the Bot.

Use of Discord itself is governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Ghostface is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements.

Ghostface is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ghostface develops, including when new features or data practices are introduced.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the Privacy Policy was most recently revised.

12. Contact

Questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests can be directed to the Ghostface Bot developer through the official Ghostface Discord server.
